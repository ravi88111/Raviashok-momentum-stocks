# Intraday provider approval checklist

Do not activate a public intraday connector until all are confirmed in writing:
- NSE and BSE coverage, including SME securities where available.
- Explicit permission for public display to unauthenticated visitors.
- Redistribution rights, not merely personal API access.
- Permitted refresh frequency, caching, historical retention and attribution.
- Delayed versus real-time classification and required labels.
- Rate limits and permitted number of concurrent users.
- Provider/exchange fees and any per-user display fees.

Until then, show daily EOD signals only and label intraday as unavailable.
